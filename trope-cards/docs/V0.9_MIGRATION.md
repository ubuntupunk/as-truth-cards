# v0.9 Migration

Adds:
- evidence_items
- evidence_sources
- evidence_claims
- evidence_interpretations
- evidence_inferences

Migration is additive. Existing source and claim records remain valid.

Validation requirements:
1. Every evidence item must identify a source.
2. Located evidence should carry a locator.
3. Evidence relation is explicit.
4. Evidence status cannot silently overwrite claim epistemic status.
5. A quotation must identify its source and location.
6. An inference may cite evidence without promoting its conclusion to ESTABLISHED.
