# Antisemitism & Anti-Zionism Trope Card Deck
*Working draft — compiled from research and ongoing development*

## Framework

The deck is organized along two crossed dimensions:

**Suits** (where a trope comes from / what era it belongs to):
1. **Classic Tropes** — pre-1948 lineage, largely unchanged in structure, retargeted at Israel/Zionists
2. **Zionism-Coded Tropes** — post-1948, intensified sharply since October 2023, reframed around "Zionist" rather than "Jew"
3. **Regional Cases (South Africa)** — specific, documented, ongoing South African proceedings; strongest evidentiary cards because they are court-tested, not merely alleged
4. **Fact-Rebuttal Reference** — argumentative/citation cards drawing on published research, functioning as the deck's evidentiary backbone rather than trope-naming cards in their own right

**Axes** (how a card functions, independent of suit):
- **Tactic-Naming** — identifies a rhetorical move, independent of whether any specific factual claim is true or false
- **Fact-Rebuttal** — makes a testable factual claim, then rebuts it with evidence
- **Theological** — rests on a contested reading of scripture or doctrine rather than an empirical claim

## SUIT ONE: Classic Tropes

**Shylock Card** — *Tactic*
Synonymous with Shakespeare's moneylender. Alleges Jews are inherently deceptive in business or predatory lenders charging usury. Predates capitalism itself, rooted in medieval Christian Europe's exclusion of Jews from land ownership and guilds, which funnelled them into finance — then blamed them for the role antisemitic law had forced on them.

**Mossad Agent Card** — *Tactic*
Alleges covert membership in Israel's intelligence service. Its function is rhetorical: recasts an opponent's presence in a debate as clandestine infiltration rather than legitimate participation, making disagreement itself look sinister. A direct descendant of older "hidden hand" conspiracy tropes about Jewish covert power.

**Zio Nazi Card** — *Tactic*
Inverts the history of Jewish persecution to draw an equivalence between the Third Reich and the current Israeli government. Strips Jews of the moral standing conferred by the Holocaust and repurposes Holocaust memory as a weapon against them — Holocaust inversion.

**World Domination Card** — *Tactic*
Alleges Jews (or "Zionists") secretly control banks, media, or governments toward a hidden agenda. The oldest and most persistent modern antisemitic myth, descended directly from the forged *Protocols of the Elders of Zion*. Used to blame Jews for everything from the Napoleonic Wars to the Rothschild banking myths to, more recently, COVID-19 and TikTok legislation.

**Dual Loyalty Card** — *Tactic*
Suggests Jews' primary allegiance lies with each other, with Israel, or with a "Jewish agenda" rather than with the country they live in. Casts Jewish citizens as permanently suspect — used since antiquity to justify exclusion, expulsion, and violence.

**Blood Libel Card** — *Tactic*
Historically the accusation that Jews murder non-Jewish children for ritual purposes. Its modern form drops the ritual framing but keeps the structure: since October 2023, social media has seen a documented surge in claims that "Zionists" enjoy killing children or celebrate Palestinian child deaths — a direct rhetorical echo of the medieval original.

**Holocaust Denial/Distortion Card** — *Fact-Rebuttal*
Either denies the genocide of six million Jews occurred, minimises its scale, or — in its more common modern form — distorts it by drawing false moral equivalence with Israeli policy. Denial and distortion serve the same function: removing the historical basis for Jewish claims to safety and self-determination.

**Talmud Card** — *Tactic / borderline Theological*
Treats the Talmud — a centuries-spanning record of rabbinic debate, disagreement, and minority opinion on how to interpret Torah law — as though it were a single, binding operational manual, then quotes isolated, decontextualized lines as proof of a hidden Jewish doctrine. The Talmud explicitly preserves *dissenting* opinions alongside majority rulings; treating any one line as "what Judaism teaches" misrepresents a text whose entire structure is argument, not decree. A direct descendant of medieval Christian Europe's Talmud trials and book-burnings.

**Chosen People / Master Race Card** — *Tactic*
Recasts the theological concept of Jewish "chosenness" — being chosen for covenant obligation, not superiority — as a supremacist claim akin to Nazi racial ideology. Inverts a concept about responsibility into one about domination.

**Havaara/ "Blood for Goods" Card** — *Tactic, ties to Zio Nazi*
Cites WWII-era negotiations between Jewish rescue committees and the SS (the Kastner case and Havaara Agreement) as proof of Zionist collusion with Nazism. Ignores the documented context of "choiceless choices" faced by Jewish leaders attempting to save lives under Nazi occupation, and functions as a variant of Holocaust inversion aimed specifically at delegitimizing Zionism's origins.

**Elders of Zion Card**
This is one of the classic conspiratorial frauds harking back to a text under Napolean, forged by the Csar to blame Jews for bolshevism.[see local cases in South Africa involving publication of the Protocols, also Durban conference]

**Jews are Not White Card**
This is one of the often repeated claims made by racists see Nuremberg Laws.

## SUIT TWO: Zionism-Coded Tropes

**Goy Card** — *Theological*
Reframes the Hebrew term for "nation" (*goy*), used repeatedly for the Israelites themselves — Genesis 12:2 promises Abraham he will become a "great nation" (*goy gadol*). Users of this reframing are usually disputing the Hebrew Covenant itself and often edge toward Replacement Theology. Most Jews use "Gentile" for non-Jews, from the Latin *gentilis* — belonging to a nation, clan, or tribe.

**Replacement Theology Card** — *Theological*
Disputes the Hebrew Covenant's continuing validity, asserting it has been superseded — theologically via Christian supersessionism, or in Islamic replacement framings via claims that later revelation supersedes the Torah. Applied politically, denies Jewish indigeneity or continuity in the land.

**Apartheid Collaborators Card** — *Fact-Rebuttal*
Singles out Israel as uniquely complicit in apartheid South Africa's survival. The US supplied nuclear-adjacent technology via Atoms for Peace, IBM supplied targeting systems, Britain's ICL did the same, France supplied Mirage jets. Jewish South Africans were also disproportionately prominent in the anti-apartheid movement — Bernstein, Wolpe, Goldberg, and Goldreich were jailed alongside Mandela in the Rivonia Trial. Israel severed remaining military ties with apartheid South Africa in 1987, with then-foreign minister Shimon Peres publicly denouncing the system at the time.

**White, European Settlers Card** — *Fact-Rebuttal*
Casts even Yemenite or Ethiopian Jews as "white European settlers" who should "go back to Poland" — a country whose Jewish population was almost entirely exterminated at Auschwitz. Over half of Israelis are Jews of Middle Eastern, North African, or other non-European descent. Assigning a single race to a religiously and ethnically diverse people, to force a false apartheid analogy, is itself a form of racial essentialism.

**Settler-Colonialism Card** — *Fact-Rebuttal, distinct from White European Settlers*
Applies the academic framework of settler-colonialism (a colonial power importing a permanent settler population to displace an indigenous one) to Israel's founding, while eliding that no independent Jewish colonial metropole existed pre-1948, and eliding continuous Jewish presence in the land across millennia. The framework was developed for cases like Australia and the Americas and requires an external "mother country" — a structural feature the Zionist movement's own history complicates.

**Zionist-as-Slur Card** — *Tactic*
Since October 2023, "Zionist" — someone who believes in Jewish self-determination — has increasingly been used as a codeword for "Jew," letting users route around platform hate-speech rules while still targeting Jewish people collectively, often paired with dehumanising language. Is usually followed by justification, “I’m only against Zionists, not Jews as such”, but its impact is still felt as Anti-Semitism, as it traverses similar themes.

**Litmus Test / Deplatforming Card** — *Tactic*
A person's actual or assumed "Zionist" beliefs are used to justify exclusion, boycott, or reputational attack — regardless of what they've actually said or done. Thus a secularist who merely supports secularism, or an economic liberal who opposes centralisation, is recast as a radical Zionist regardless of ones outlook or position in the conflict. **Platform/Institutional Control Card** — *Tactic*
Alleges "Zionist" influence covertly shapes a platform, university, or piece of legislation — e.g. claims that a 2024 US TikTok bill was secretly engineered by Jewish lobbying. Structurally identical to the *Protocols of the Elders of Zion* — only the institution named has changed.

**River to Sea Card** — *Theological/contested*
"From the river to the sea, Palestine will be free." Supporters read it as a call for Palestinian freedom from occupation; critics read it as a call for Israel's elimination, given the phrase's origin in PLO and Hamas charters predating any peace-process framing. Note: "Greater Israel" maximalists use a mirror-image phrase for the opposite claim — the slogan's ambiguity is itself part of the dispute.

**Weaponizing Antisemitism Card** — *Tactic*
Claims that antisemitism accusations are themselves deployed in bad faith to silence legitimate criticism of Israel. The meta-move that pre-empts every other card in this deck — it doesn't argue any specific point is wrong, it argues the whole framework of identifying tropes is illegitimate.

## SUIT THREE: South Africa — Regional Cases

**Cape Union Mart Card** — *Fact-Rebuttal*
Since November 2023, the Palestine Solidarity Campaign protested outside Cape Union Mart stores nationwide, claiming the retailer and its chairman, Philip Krawitz, were "funding genocide" and complicit in killing children in Gaza. In September 2026, a full bench of the Western Cape High Court found the respondents had failed to establish a factual basis and relied heavily on hearsay, granting a final interdict barring the specific claims — while explicitly leaving the boycott and protest itself intact and stating the ruling was not a pronouncement on the Gaza war. PSC has since sought leave to appeal. *[status: live litigation — revisit on appeal outcome]*

**UCT Resolutions Card** — *Tactic-Naming + Fact-Rebuttal*
In June 2024, UCT's Council passed resolutions endorsing a boycott of Israel and rejecting the IHRA definition of antisemitism. Professor Adam Mendelsohn, Director of UCT's Kaplan Centre for Jewish Studies, is challenging the process in the Cape High Court — per his own affidavits, not contesting Zionism or the Gaza war itself, but arguing the Council acted outside its fiduciary duties and infringed academic freedom. *[status: ongoing — revisit on judgment]*

**SA Jews for a Free Palestine Card** — *Tactic*
Illustrates using Jewish identity as a shield against an antisemitism accusation, on the logic that a Jewish speaker cannot by definition perpetuate antisemitic tropes. A member of this group publicly questioned why Mendelsohn should be "allowed a voice" after his UCT inaugural lecture, calling his objections "Zionist paranoia" — statements he is now suing over for defamation. *[status: live litigation]*

## SUIT FOUR: Fact-Rebuttal Reference Cards
*(drawn from "Everything You've Been Told About the Palestinian Struggle is Wrong," medialternatives.com)*

**Jerusalem Sovereignty Card**
East Jerusalem "capital of Palestine" claims typically omit that Jordan occupied and administered East Jerusalem 1948–1967, during which it barred Jews from the Western Wall and destroyed multiple historic synagogues and yeshivas dating back centuries.

**Quran/Land Claim Card** — *Theological*
Replacement-theology arguments citing Al-Aqsa's sanctity to claim exclusive Arab/Muslim entitlement to the land elide that the Quran contains no reference to "Palestine," but several passages (e.g. Al Baqara 2:47, Al Ma'ida 5:21) describing the land as granted to the Jews.

**The "Apartheid Map" Card**
The widely circulated meme map showing Israeli "land theft" over time drops the 1949 Armistice line, ignores the parallel displacement of Jews from Arab countries, and confuses demographic change with territorial annexation.

**Voting Rights Card**
Claims that "Arab Israelis cannot vote" conflate Arab citizens of Israel (who vote in the Knesset) with residents of the Palestinian Authority (who vote, when elections are held, for the PNA/PA separately) — two different political entities.

**Nakba Symmetry Card**
Nakba figures are frequently cited without the parallel expulsion of roughly 850,000 Jews from Arab and Muslim countries in the same period.

**Balfour/Colonial Origins Card**
Framing Israel's founding purely through the 1917 Balfour Declaration omits the earlier McMahon-Hussein Correspondence, the Sykes-Picot Agreement, the 1918 Ottoman Declaration, and the Weizmann-Faisal Agreement — a denser and more contested diplomatic history than the single-document narrative suggests.

**Palestinian Flag Card**
The flag flown at pro-Palestinian rallies is the Pan-Arab flag, shared with Jordan, the Syrian Ba'ath Party, and others, tracing to the 1917 Flag of the Arab Revolt rather than to any Palestine-specific origin.

**Chronology Card**
The First Zionist Congress (1897) preceded organized Palestinian nationalism by roughly three decades; the framing of Zionism as the "secondary," reactive movement inverts the documented chronology.

**ANC/Hamas Equivalence Card**
Draws on Mandela's own 1990 statement to Ted Koppel that ANC support for Arafat never extended to doubting Israel's legal right to exist — complicates the ANC-Palestinian-struggle analogy asserted uncritically in South African activist spaces.

**Israel–Apartheid Severance Card**
Israel severed remaining military ties with apartheid South Africa in 1987, with then-foreign minister Shimon Peres publicly denouncing the system at the time.

**False Binary / "Choose Sides" Card**
The demand that observers "pick a side" or be complicit, modeled explicitly on the anti-apartheid struggle's moral clarity, is argued to misapply a binary racial-justice framework to a religio-national conflict where both "sides" have committed documented injustices.

Depo-Provera Card — Fact-Rebuttal, contested — flag for care
Raised when Ethiopian Jewish repatriation (Operations Magic Carpet, Moses, Solomon) comes up as evidence of Israel's racial diversity, met with the counter-claim that Israel ran a covert population-control program against Ethiopian-Israeli women via forced Depo-Provera injections. The record is mixed: disproportionate administration and informed-consent failures were real and admitted by Israel's Health Ministry in 2013, prompting corrective clinical directives — but no investigation has substantiated a deliberate policy of ethnically targeted birth-rate suppression, and the state comptroller's inquiry (itself criticized for not interviewing complainants) found no evidence of coercion under threat. The ~50% fertility decline among Ethiopian-Israeli women is separately explained by urbanization, education, and delayed childbearing — matching patterns seen in other populations undergoing rapid socioeconomic transition. Use with care: this card documents a genuine, acknowledged medical-ethics failure, not a fabricated trope — the deck should distinguish "this happened and was wrong" from "this proves a genocidal population policy," rather than dismissing the whole matter.
